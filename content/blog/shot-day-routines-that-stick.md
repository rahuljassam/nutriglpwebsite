## Start with fewer decisions

The easiest routine is the one you do not have to reinvent every week. Pick the same time, the same place, and the same checklist so shot day feels automatic.

## Keep the setup visible

Put the items you need in one spot before the day arrives. When supplies are ready, follow-through gets much easier.

## Finish with a tiny note

Write down what happened right after your shot. A quick note about timing, energy, or appetite can make the next week easier to plan.

The goal is not perfection. The goal is a repeatable system that supports consistency.
