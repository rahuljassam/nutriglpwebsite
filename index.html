Core workflow

Everything important, organized in one place.

NutriGLP helps people turn weekly medication into a repeatable routine they can actually keep.

Medication tracking

  • Log each shot with timing, dose, and injection site.
  • See a 7-day medication level forecast for every cycle.
  • Use reminders so the routine stays consistent week after week.

Nutrition support

  • Track protein, hydration, fiber, and meal timing in the same flow.
  • Use meal planning guidance that fits your medication schedule.
  • Notice how your hunger and energy patterns shift through the week.

Progress visibility

  • Monitor weight and trend lines with less spreadsheet work.
  • Reflect on consistency across habits instead of single-day wins.
  • Keep the signals that matter in view without overcomplicating the process.
